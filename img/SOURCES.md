# Image sources

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. On the site they are cropped to 4:3 (the forest is not cropped), resized, and re-encoded as WebP.
The full-resolution originals are kept outside the deploy folder, in `/workspace/fjyang-site/img-originals/`.
The credits line in the page footer covers the attribution these licenses require.
